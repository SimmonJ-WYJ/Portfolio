# SimmonJ Homepage Copy & Interaction Spec

> Version: v1.0  
> Page: simmonj.com Homepage  
> Positioning: AI Product Designer  
> Goal: Present Simmon’s product design experience, AI product background, and 0→1 capability in a clear, human, and credible way, then distill the story into five memorable core capabilities through scroll interaction.

---

## 1. Page Goal

The homepage should answer these questions quickly:

1. Who is Simmon?
2. What is his current positioning?
3. What makes him different from a generic Product Designer?
4. What AI and 0→1 products has he worked on?
5. How does he actually work?
6. What five capabilities should visitors remember at the end?

Avoid generic design language such as “creating meaningful experiences,” “user-centered innovation,” or other statements that could apply to almost any designer.

Core principles:

- Less abstract design philosophy, more real working process
- Less “what I believe,” more “how I work”
- Make AI product experience part of the main narrative
- Naturally surface data, 0→1 work, AI workflow validation, and engineering collaboration
- End the capabilities section with five strong, memorable keywords

# 5. ABOUT / HOW I WORK

## 5.1 Section Title

# I usually don’t start with the interface.

---

## 5.2 Main Copy

Before opening Figma, I want to understand what’s actually going wrong.

Where are people dropping off?

Why does the current flow feel difficult?

Is it an interaction problem, a product problem, or is the AI simply not capable enough yet?

I use user feedback, product analytics, and funnels to find those problems.

For AI features, I often prototype the workflow first to see whether the model can actually complete the task before turning it into a product experience.

Once the idea works, I move into interaction, interface, and implementation.

**Understand → Test → Design → Ship → Learn.**

That’s how I approach product design today.

# 7. CORE CAPABILITIES INTERACTION

## Stage 01 — Full Narrative

- Display the full body copy
- Maintain relatively high information density
- Project imagery can appear behind or between the text
- All text starts in regular weight

---

## Stage 02 — Keyword Emphasis

As the user scrolls, the following words gradually become stronger:

- PRODUCT THINKING
- AI-NATIVE
- DATA-DRIVEN
- 0 → 1
- END-TO-END

Non-keyword copy gradually loses contrast.

---

## Stage 03 — Supporting Copy Fades

As scrolling continues:

- Supporting copy fades out
- Images reduce in prominence or leave the frame
- Core capability words remain
- Keywords can detach from their original inline positions and transition into a larger editorial layout

---

## Stage 04 — Final Capability State

The screen ends with only:

**PRODUCT THINKING**  
**AI-NATIVE**  
**DATA-DRIVEN**  
**0 → 1**  
**END-TO-END**

In Chinese mode, use the corresponding Chinese terms.

The final state should feel like a summary of “what Simmon brings,” not another paragraph to read.

---

## 7.1 Motion Principles

- Avoid overly complex animation
- No random floating effects
- Motion should communicate hierarchy
- The narrative is “explanation → emphasis → summary”
- Opacity, font weight, position, and scale are enough
- Avoid excessive spring effects
- Keep the interaction restrained, modern, and editorial

# 11. COPY TONE

The homepage should feel:

- Human
- Professional without sounding over-written
- Opinionated without becoming manifesto-like
- Short and direct
- Grounded in real work
- Product-oriented rather than purely visual
- AI-native without using AI as decoration

Avoid:

- “Empowering users”
- “Creating meaningful experiences”
- “Bridging humans and technology”
- “Innovative solutions”
- Generic design-manifesto language

---

# 12. DESIGN & DEVELOPMENT ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

## Copy

- The Hero should communicate the positioning within 10 seconds
- About should not repeat the Hero
- All five capability keywords must be supported by surrounding copy
- Metrics must remain consistent with case studies
- Chinese and English versions should communicate the same meaning without requiring literal translation

## Interaction

- The capabilities section must clearly transition from narrative → emphasis → summary
- The final five keywords need a distinct visual state
- Mobile must not depend on hover
- Mobile can simplify the sequence to sticky + fade
- Reduced Motion mode should provide a clear static fallback

## Visual

- High-contrast black and white
- Generous whitespace
- No gradients as a primary visual device
- Typography-led layout
- Imagery should support projects and narrative
- Avoid stereotypical blue-purple “AI” visuals
